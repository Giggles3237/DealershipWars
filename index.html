Dealership Wars Simulator

A playable browser prototype for building, protecting, and sabotaging deals.

Four local players rotate clockwise, teams share profit, completed deals survive to the next allied turn, and every card comes from the 54-card JSON deck.

Drag cards straight onto the table, let the AI handle the other seats, and use the deck library below as your live rules reference while the prototype runs.
